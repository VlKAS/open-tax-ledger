How to export the right IBKR statement
  1. In Client Portal, open Performance & Reports → Statements.
  2. Run an Activity Statement with trades, dividends, withholding, positions, and transfers.
  3. Choose CSV and include both FY and calendar-year coverage when available.

Conservative first scope

  • IBKR Activity Statement CSV section parsing
  • Foreign-listed equity and ETF source rows
  • Dividends, withholding, interest, and open positions
  • Draft CG / FSI / TR / FA review tables
  • Pinned community USD TT BUY table and local CSV override
  • Offline SEC company, ticker, exchange, and CIK associations
  • Validation findings and source audit counts
Flagged, not guessed

Outside the current engine

  • Direct ITR or Form 67 submission
  • Options, futures, shorts, bonds, forex, or business-income classification
  • Corporate actions and transferred-in lots with missing basis
  • An official historical SBI archive or automatic Rule 115 date selection
  • Final tax liability or professional advice
